2. Paylaşım

Verilerini satmayız. Yalnızca oyunu çalıştırmak için gereken hizmet sağlayıcılarla paylaşılır: sunucu barındırma (Heroku / Microsoft Azure), reklam (Unity LevelPlay ve aracı reklam ağları), çökme raporlama (Sentry), uygulama mağazaları (Google, Apple). Diğer oyuncular yalnızca kaptan adını, gemi seviyeni ve maç içi istatistiklerini görür.

3. Reklamlar

Oyunda reklam yalnızca sen istediğinde (ödüllü reklam) gösterilir. İlk açılışta kişiselleştirilmiş reklam için onayın istenir; reddedersen kişiselleştirilmemiş reklam gösterilir. iOS'ta izin vermediğin sürece reklam kimliğine erişilmez.

4. Saklama ve silme

Verilerin hesabın açık kaldığı sürece saklanır. Oyunda Ayarlar → Hesap → Hesabımı sil ile hesabını ve oyun verilerini kalıcı olarak silebilirsin; yasal zorunluluk olan satın alma kayıtları anonim olarak ve yalnızca gereken süre boyunca tutulur. Silme talebini e-postayla da iletebilirsin.

5. Çocuklar

Oyun 13 yaşın altındaki çocuklara yönelik değildir ve bu yaştaki çocuklardan bilerek veri toplamayız.

6. Hakların (KVKK / GDPR)

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data Sail & Raid ("the Game"), developed by Wolk Interactive ("we"), collects, why, and how it is protected.

1. Data we collect

  • Game account: a randomly generated account ID and secret key (stored on our server only as a hash) and the captain name you choose — to save your progress.
  • Gameplay data: match results, statistics, recent match history, quests and Trophy Road progress, purchase and reward records — to run the game, grant rewards correctly, and investigate cheating and refunds.
  • Connection data: IP address and ping — to connect you to a server and prevent abuse (kept briefly in server logs).
  • Advertising ID (only if you watch a rewarded ad; via Unity LevelPlay) — to show ads and verify rewards.
  • Crash and error reports (device model, OS version, error log) — to fix bugs.
  • Google / Apple account link (optional: account ID and email) — to save your account and restore it on another device.

We do not collect your real name, phone number, location, contacts, camera or microphone. Payments are processed by Google Play / the App Store; we never see card details.

2. Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with service providers needed to run the Game: server hosting (Heroku / Microsoft Azure), advertising (Unity LevelPlay and mediated ad networks), crash reporting (Sentry), and the app stores (Google, Apple). Other players only see your captain name, ship level and in-match statistics.

3. Ads

Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one (rewarded ads). On first launch you are asked to consent to personalised ads; if you decline, non-personalised ads are shown. On iOS the advertising ID is not accessed unless you allow tracking.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and game data in Settings → Account → Delete my account; purchase records required by law are kept anonymised and only as long as required. You may also request deletion by email.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

You have the right to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your data. Contact: destek@wolkstudio.com

7. Changes

If we update this policy we will change the date above and announce significant changes in the Game.
